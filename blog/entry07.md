## Skills
The two skills I learned over the course of completing my Freedom Project was commmunication andcCreativity. 

### Communication
When presenting in front of my class, I was able to get practice and prepare for the presentation in front of the judges. I practiced what I was going to say in the order I was going to say it. For instance, I would start off with my hook, next I show off my website and its features, then present my Part A and finish with showing my product, the Screen Pen. Also, I was able to practice speaking with confidence and energy to keep my classmates and judges engaged. Finally, this improved my communication skills in front of my peers and adults. 

### Creativity

I used my creativity to create the Screen Pen. I didn't want my product to be similar to other inventions or possibly replaced by AI. So, I thought of creating a pen that would force kids to use their brains and get the answers on their own, instead of using AI where they can just get the answers and don't learn anything on how the answer is correct. My Screen Pen shows kids the grammatical errors they are making, but doesn't give them the corrrect answer, the kids have to figrue out what the answer is and why its right. Overall,  I learned how to channel my creativity in order to make a product that stood out, and I was successful because I recieved a compliment from the judges that my invention was very unique.
