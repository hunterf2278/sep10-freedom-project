# SEP10 Freedom Project
by **Hunter-Rose Forrester**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for SEP10 is a year-long project all about making a website that informs the viewer of the current and future innovations in the topic of my choosing.

For my project, I chose the topic of **Writing**. 

I used HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and Github. I also chose to independently study **Aframe, a 3D modeling tool** in order to help me make my website. Then I used that tool  to make my product, a technology that can help students improve their writing.  Finally, we had to create our own website using two bootstrap components that we learned, an then present our website and product to judges at the Expo. 

Now, the product I made was the Screen Pe  which is an electric pen with an eraser and a screen.  While people write, the screen shows the sentence, then underlines the word and flahses red to indicate that the student made a grammatical error. It flashes blue to tell the perosn they mispelled a word. The Screen pen only shows you the error, the student has to figure out why its wrong and whats the correct answer. This way, students are forced to use their brains and solve the porblem themselves. Instead, of using chatgpt where they get the answer and don't learn anything from it.
